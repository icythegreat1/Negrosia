About Negrosia

Negrosia is an online marketplace created to showcase locally made arts and crafts. It provides local craft sellers with a space to present their handmade products and allows customers to discover unique creations in one convenient platform.

From woven crafts and handmade accessories to bags, home decor, gifts, and souvenirs, Negrosia aims to make local craftsmanship more accessible to customers.
